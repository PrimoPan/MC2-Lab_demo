Professor Pan Hui

Chair Professor, Center for Metaverse and Computational Creativity (MC2)

International Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering, Member of Academia Europaea, and IEEE Fellow.

Official Homepage

Biography

Professor Pan Hui leads MC2 at HKUST(GZ) and HKUST. His research spans metaverse systems, social computing, computational social science, and human-centered AI, with a focus on impactful interdisciplinary innovation.

Research Focus

  • Immersive technologies (VR/AR/XR/MR) and metaverse infrastructure
  • Social computing and prosocial interaction design
  • Generative AI and large language model applications
  • Cross-disciplinary AI+ education and digital creativity

Selected Highlights

  • Publications in top-tier venues across systems, HCI, AI, and digital arts
  • Large-scale cross-campus immersive education initiatives
  • Leadership in interdisciplinary collaborations bridging academia and industry
